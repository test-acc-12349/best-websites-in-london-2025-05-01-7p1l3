Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical website development timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks, following our proven 5-phase development process. Complex e-commerce sites may require 8-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What ongoing support do you provide after launch?

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including 24/7 monitoring, security updates, content updates, and technical support. Our dedicated support team ensures your site remains secure and performs optimally.

How do you handle website security?

Security is our top priority. We implement SSL certificates, regular security audits, automated backups, and advanced firewall protection. All our sites include real-time threat monitoring and malware scanning.

What's included in your website packages?

Our packages include custom design, responsive development, SEO optimization, content management system, performance optimization, analytics integration, and comprehensive training. We also provide 30 days of post-launch support.

Do you offer e-commerce solutions?

Yes, we specialize in building secure, scalable e-commerce websites using platforms like WooCommerce and Shopify. Our solutions include payment gateway integration, inventory management, and order fulfillment systems.
